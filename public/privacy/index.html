Privacy

Privacy notice

What we collect, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

When you pledge we collect your full name, email, street address, neighborhood and amount. Payments are processed by Stripe; we receive confirmation and the amount, never your card number.

What is public

Only your first name and last initial (or “Anonymous neighbor”), your neighborhood, and amounts. Your full name, address and email are never published.

Who can see private details

Only the fund's treasurer and team members they appoint, who sign in with a one-time code. A developer invited to fix a technical problem can't see personal details. Access is enforced by the database itself, and every change is logged.

How we use it

To confirm local residency, match payments to pledges, contact you about the fund, and return money if the fund closes. We never sell or share your information, except where required by law.

Giving plans

If you choose smaller payments over time, Stripe stores your card and takes each payment on schedule. We record each payment, never the card. You can stop a plan at any time from your private link.

Security logging

To protect the fund, staff sign-ins and changes, and attempts to use private links or staff pages without permission, are recorded in a security log. Internet addresses are kept only as a scrambled fingerprint that can't be turned back into the address.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are turned on, they are cookie-free and count page views without identifying you.

Your choices

Email the treasurer to see, correct or delete your information. Records of money received must be kept for accounting and legal purposes.
