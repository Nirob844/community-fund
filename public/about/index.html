About us

Neighbors from five communities, one voice for responsible growth

SMART Manatee, the Southern Manatee Association for Responsible Transition, was formed in October 2026 by residents from RioMar, Magnolia Point, The Trails, Quail Run, and Fiddlers Creek. Two also serve on their HOA boards.

Our mission (working draft)

To advocate for responsible, properly scaled development that respects neighborhood character, protects local infrastructure, and opposes high-density projects that compromise community livability.

Where we work

Southern Manatee County: south of State Road 70, east of U.S. 301, west of I-75, and north to the county line at University Parkway. Putting "Manatee" in our name ties us to the wider Suncoast and leaves room to help neighbors anywhere in the county, because responsible development is smart everywhere in Manatee County.

What is infill development?

Infill means building on green space in areas where roads, stormwater systems, utilities, schools and emergency medical services are already in place. The question we ask about every project is simple: can those systems carry what is proposed, without pushing flooding, traffic or response times onto the neighbors already here?

Our first focus: Prospect Road

The board's immediate focus is the high-density project being expedited under Florida's Live Local Act at 6991 to 7065 Prospect Road: about 640 apartments on 29.68 acres zoned for agriculture, in the Rattlesnake Slough drainage basin, near an active bald eagle nest. Read our updates on the blog.

Governance and legal help

The board is putting the nonprofit's governing documents in place with help from the law firm selected to represent SMART Manatee. We will introduce the attorney once the board has signed the retainer agreement.
