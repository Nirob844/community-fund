Prospect Road Community Fund is organized by volunteer residents of Manatee County, Florida. Contributions are not tax-deductible unless and until the organization receives 501(c)(3) recognition or uses a fiscal sponsor. Statements are prepared by the volunteer treasurer and are unaudited. If the fund closes, any remaining balance is returned in proportion to contributions unless contributors agree otherwise. This site is not legal advice.
