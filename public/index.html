      Why this fund exists

      A 642-unit apartment plan has been filed for about 29 acres on Prospect Road under Florida's Live Local Act, which lets qualifying projects skip a public hearing. Residents believe the land does not qualify and are asking Manatee County to correct its eligibility finding. The fund pays for a Florida land use attorney, expert review and public records. If the property ever becomes available, the fund's long-term target is to buy it and create a park for the whole community.

      Questions

      What is the Prospect Road Community Fund?

      A volunteer-run fund organized by residents near Prospect Road and Tuttle Avenue in Manatee County, Florida. It pays for a land use attorney and related costs to ask the county to correct its finding that the land qualifies for the Live Local Act, and it may help buy the land for a community park.

      How is my money used?

      Every dollar received and every expense is published on the Transparency page, with an income statement and balance sheet. Only the treasurer can record entries, and every change is logged.

      What happens to money that isn't spent?

      If the fund closes with a balance, it is returned to contributors in proportion to what each gave. If two people each give $100 and $100 is spent, each gets $50 back.

      What is shown publicly about me?

      Only your first name and last initial (or “Anonymous neighbor”) and your neighborhood. Your full name, street address and email are private to the treasurer.

      Is my payment secure?

      Card payments are processed by Stripe on its own secure page. This site never sees or stores card numbers.

      Why would the fund buy the land?

      If the property becomes available, a fund large enough to match its agricultural value could make an offer and turn it into a park for the whole community. The milestone scale shows progress toward that $2,000,000 target.

      Are contributions tax-deductible?

      Not yet. They become tax-deductible only if the organization receives 501(c)(3) recognition from the IRS or works through a fiscal sponsor.
