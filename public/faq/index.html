Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions neighbors most frequently ask about SMART Manatee, our legal defense fund, the Prospect Road development proposal, and how every dollar is handled.

About SMART Manatee & Our Mission

What is SMART Manatee?

SMART Manatee (Southern Manatee Association for Responsible Transition) is a volunteer-led nonprofit organized in October 2026 by residents across five neighborhoods—RioMar, Magnolia Point, The Trails, Quail Run, and Fiddlers Creek. We advocate for properly scaled growth, flood prevention, and protection of local infrastructure and wildlife.

Is SMART Manatee against new housing?

No. We support smart, responsible development that aligns with existing stormwater infrastructure, road capacity, schools, and emergency services. High-density infill on agricultural land without required hydrological upgrades poses severe flooding risks to neighboring homes.

Who runs the organization and makes decisions?

SMART Manatee is governed by an all-volunteer board of local residents, including HOA board officers and long-time neighborhood homeowners. No one receives a salary or executive compensation. Retained land use attorneys and environmental engineers work directly on behalf of our collective communities.

How can I help besides donating?

You can attend our upcoming online town halls, share our factual research with your neighbors, and help document site conditions (such as standing water, flooding, or drill rigs) from public roadways.

The Prospect Road Proposal & The Live Local Act

What is happening on Prospect Road?

About 640 apartments across multiple 4-story buildings are proposed on 29.68 acres currently zoned for agriculture (A-1) at 6991 to 7065 Prospect Road (County record PLN2607-0110). The site is directly in the Rattlesnake Slough drainage basin and near an active bald eagle nest.

What is the Live Local Act?

The Live Local Act is a Florida state statute intended to incentivize affordable workforce housing by allowing qualifying projects to proceed without traditional county rezoning hearings and County Commission public votes. Because administrative approval skips the usual public hearing process, neighbors must present formal legal and hydrological challenges directly to county staff.

Why is stormwater drainage and flooding such a critical concern?

During Hurricane Debby in August 2024, emergency personnel carried out high-water rescues from 14 homes near Whitfield and Prospect, evacuating 32 adults, 12 children, and 13 pets. Replacing 30 acres of agricultural watershed with impermeable rooftops and asphalt will displace millions of gallons of water directly into Rattlesnake Slough.

What wildlife protections are at issue?

An active bald eagle nest is located adjacent to the site. Florida's bald eagle nesting season extends from October 1 through May 15. Construction, tree clearing, and heavy equipment operation within the 660-foot federal buffer zone are subject to strict U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service guidelines.

The Community Legal Fund & Financial Transparency

How is my donation money used?

Every dollar contributed goes directly to specialized land use legal representation, hydrological and traffic engineering reviews, and public records requests. Contributions are kept in our dedicated fund bank account, and invoices are approved by the board.

What is the 100% Proportional Refund Guarantee?

If the legal fund achieves its goals or closes with an unspent balance, the remaining funds will be returned to contributors in direct proportion to what each gave. For instance, if the campaign finishes with 40% of funds unspent, every contributor receives 40% of their money back.

How do I know my money is secure?

Payments are processed using Stripe's bank-grade encrypted checkout. This website never sees, collects, or stores your credit card number or bank details.

What personal details are shown on the public ledger?

Transparency is balanced with personal privacy. Only your first name and last initial (or “Anonymous neighbor” if preferred) and your neighborhood appear on the public ledger. Your full name, street address, and email are strictly private to the volunteer treasurer.

Are contributions tax-deductible?

Contributions to SMART Manatee are not tax-deductible unless and until 501(c)(3) recognition is finalized. Florida charitable solicitation registration is currently pending.

Why is there a $2,000,000 community park milestone?

If legal challenges or market conditions lead the property owner to seek an alternative, our long-term community vision is to partner with county conservation programs and land trusts to acquire the parcel at agricultural appraisal value and establish a permanent passive community park and stormwater preserve.
